# Tenpin bundled samples: sources and licences

Every sample in this folder is either public domain (CC0 1.0) or made for Tenpin and
dedicated to the public domain. No attribution is legally required, but the authors are
credited below. The licence was checked on each source page (Freesound sound page or the
source repository's LICENSE file) in November 2026.

How the files were made: each WAV is the listed source (several sources means mic
channels of one hit mixed together), converted to 44.1 kHz 16-bit mono, peak-normalised
to -1 dBFS, leading silence cut at -50 dBFS, tail cut once it stays under -60 dBFS,
then given a short fade-out. Files are capped at 2 s (4 s for subs, rides and crashes).
Freesound originals need a login, so the Freesound HQ previews (Ogg Vorbis) were used
where no lossless copy was available. Sonic Pi samples are Sonic Pi's own trimmed FLAC
copies of the linked Freesound sounds.

`sub_808_*.wav` were synthesised for Tenpin with NumPy and SoX: a sine whose pitch
falls from 2.2x to the root within about 60 ms (f = f0 * (1 + 1.2 * e^(-t/25ms))),
a 2 ms attack, exponential decay and tanh saturation. `sub_808_f`: F1 (43.65 Hz),
decay tau 0.5 s, drive 1.5. `sub_808_c_drive`: C2 (65.41 Hz), tau 0.45 s, drive 5.
They are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0.
